Oral presentation at the 39th International Conference on Coastal Engineering (ICCE 2026), organized by the Coasts, Oceans, Ports, and Rivers Institute of the American Society of Civil Engineers. This work presents a sensitivity study of an impact-based infrastructure prioritization framework for coastal resilience, evaluating how equity-based restoration decisions respond to uncertainty in sociodemographic inputs across an electric distribution network. Co-author: Abigail L. Beck, University of Houston.
